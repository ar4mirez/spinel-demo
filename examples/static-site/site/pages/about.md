# About this site

This site is the output of `bin/build`, a static site generator of about 250 lines of Ruby. It reads:

- Markdown pages and posts, with YAML front matter
- ERB layouts and ERB pages
- a `config.yml` with site-wide settings

and writes plain HTML files you can serve from anywhere.

> Everything here ran on **Spinel**, not CRuby.
